## Brand & Style

This design system defines a premium, vibrant loyalty and retail rewards experience for convenience store shoppers across Mexico. The brand persona bridges everyday neighborhood familiarity with an elevated, digitally agile membership ecosystem. 

Key attributes:
- **Tone:** Accessible yet premium, energizing, rewarding, and deeply dependable.
- **Target Audience:** Everyday consumers, commuters, and loyal neighborhood shoppers seeking instant value, clear point-redemption pathways, and seamless digital checkout at physical store counters.
- **Emotional Response:** Excitement around accumulated benefits, trust in balance precision, and frictionless satisfaction during transactions.

The visual direction combines crisp retail modernism with subtle tactile feedback: generous whitespace, high-contrast typography, warm conversational accents, and card-based modularity optimized for fast scanning at the point of sale.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the signature Yepas cobalt blue (`#0043A8`), establishing authority, trust, and clarity across the digital portal. 

- **Primary (`#0043A8`):** Dominates top-level navigation, primary buttons, digital loyalty membership cards, and key interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#FFD100`):** Acts as the high-energy loyalty gold for point counters, unlocked tier statuses, dynamic reward badges, and active promotional banners.
- **Tertiary (`#E03A26`):** Provides a warm, retail-driven urgency for flash deals, expiring points, and hot offers.
- **Neutral (`#182B56`):** Replaces pure black with deep maritime navy for high-contrast, accessible typography, input borders, and dark structural accents.
- **Backgrounds:** Clean, airy compositions leveraging neutral tinted whites (`#FFFFFF` and `#F6F8FC`) to ensure optimal legibility under bright outdoor light or low store lighting.

## Typography

The type system blends the energetic geometry of **Outfit** for headlines and display figures with the workhorse legibility of **Hanken Grotesk** for core UI, body copy, and point counters.

- **Headlines & Display:** Outfit provides a contemporary, friendly geometric presence. Large balance indicators and campaign titles leverage Outfit Bold for instant recognition on mobile devices.
- **Body & Data:** Hanken Grotesk brings clarity and balanced proportion to reward transaction feeds, product specifications, and terms of service.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Point values, barcode captions, and wallet balances utilize medium-to-bold weights with tabular lining numbers to keep reward metrics strictly aligned.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 8pt fluid grid model underpins the portal:
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Focuses on single-column reward feeds, sticky bottom QR scan buttons, and swipeable promotional carousels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `margin-md: 2rem`, `gutter-md: 1.5rem`. Enables dual-column interfaces featuring the digital card on one side and active missions on the other.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxed at 1280px, `margin-lg: 3rem`, `gutter-lg: 2rem`. Balanced dashboard with persistent navigation, wallet overview, and reward catalog grids.

Spacing follows strict rhythmic density: compact internal padding (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) inside interactive pills and badges, moderate rhythm (`space-md`, `space-lg`) within content cards, and expansive offsets (`space-xl`) between distinct functional modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages soft, chromatic ambient drop shadows combined with crisp, semi-opaque borders rather than heavy blur planes:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Used for base surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#F6F8FC`). Subtle divider lines (`rgba(24, 43, 86, 0.08)`) create structure without visual clutter.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Badges):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(24, 43, 86, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(24, 43, 86, 0.04)`. Applied to offer tiles, history rows, and promotional banners.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Wallets):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 67, 168, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 43, 86, 0.04)`. Applied to the primary dynamic Loyalty Pass card and active floating action controls.
- **Level 3 (Modals & QR Code Sheets):** `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(24, 43, 86, 0.2)`. Ensures high contrast and immediate focus when generating in-store redemption barcodes.

## Shapes

With a roundedness factor of `2`:
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, inputs, reward cards) feature an 8px (`0.5rem`) base corner radius.
- Large contextual surfaces (loyalty member pass, featured promo cards, dialogue modals) scale up to 16px (`1rem`).
- Loyalty badges, status tags, and point redemption pills use complete pill radii (9999px) for friendly, touch-friendly affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0043A8`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded `0.5rem`, height 48px, font `label-lg`. Micro-transition with slight scale (0.98) on tap.
- **Secondary (Loyalty Gold):** Background `#FFD100`, text `#182B56`, rounded `0.5rem`, height 48px, font `label-lg`. Reserved for "Canjear Puntos" (Redeem Points) and instant unlock CTAs.
- **Outlined:** Border 1.5px solid `#0043A8`, text `#0043A8`, background transparent.

### Loyalty Pass & Balance Cards
- **Membership Card:** Deep cobalt `#0043A8` gradient to `#182B56` with high-contrast `#FFD100` numeric point counts. Features an embedded, tap-to-expand QR code for counter scanning with an 8px white quiet zone border.
- **Tier Progress Indicators:** Pill-shaped tracking bars with track `#E8EEF8` and fill `#FFD100`, accented by animated tier milestone badges.

### Badges & Chips
- **Category Chips:** Light container `#F6F8FC` with border `1px solid rgba(24, 43, 86, 0.1)`. Selected state transitions to solid `#0043A8` with white text.
- **Promotion Badges:** Pill shape, background `#E03A26`, text `#FFFFFF`, uppercase `label-sm` with tight letter spacing for discounts ("2x1", "-30%").

### Form Controls
- **Inputs:** 48px height, rounded `0.5rem`, surface white, border 1.5px `#D7DFEC`, text `#182B56`. Focused state shifts border to `#0043A8` with a 3px soft halo `rgba(0, 67, 168, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded `0.25rem` for checkboxes, circular for radios. Active state fills with `#0043A8` with crisp white check/dot.

### Offer & Product Cards
- Elevated at Level 1, surface white, 12px padding. Image containers use a clean 1:1 ratio with rounded `0.5rem` corners and subtle inner borders to frame store products against the canvas.
